                        At rest, service by service

                        Service At rest
                        S3Encrypted by default, switch to your own KMS key if you want
                        EBSPer disk, or "encryption by default" for the whole Region
                        RDSChosen at creation, can't be switched on later
                        Parameter StoreSecureString parameters, encrypted with a KMS key

                        Default to encrypted for every database, even the "temporary" ones.

                          Envelope encryption in four steps

                          • Ask for a data keyKMS returns it twice: in plaintext and encrypted under your KMS key
                          • Encrypt locallyFast, any size, then throw the plaintext key away
                          • Store them togetherThe encrypted data key sits right next to the encrypted data
                          • Read it backSend the encrypted key to KMS, it checks and returns the plaintext key

                            KMS disables your key. What happens to every file encrypted with it?

                              • Never in GitBots scan public repos within minutes
