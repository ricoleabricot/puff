      Your group is a small cloud provider

      Six courses of Puff, one tenant so far. Now a real customer walks in with a need and one annoying constraint.

        A customer doesn't care about your VPC. They ask four things.

        • Will it be up when I need it?Uptime, an honest promise
        • What happens when something breaks?Recovery, how fast and how much data lost
        • What does it cost me each month?A monthly price on your design
        • Is my data safe?Where it lives, who can read it

          Your team and your base Puff

          • Groups of 3, drawnYou don't choose your group, so make sure your course 5 Puff works
          • One base PuffPick the member's Puff that works best. Everything you demo runs there
          • Test elsewhere firstThe other two try risky changes in their own sandboxes, then apply them to the base
          • You defend all of itEach of you gets asked about a part you didn't build

                                      A pitch that works

                                      • 20 secondsYour customer and their twist, with numbers: how many users, when, what data
                                      • 1 minuteThe diagram, following one request from the browser to the database and back
                                      • 1 minuteThe live demo, ending on the proof you handled the twist
                                      • 10 seconds eachHow it scales, stays available when an AZ goes, recovers after a disaster

                                        Before and during your slot

                                        • Every tab openCloudFront URL, ALB address, target group, RDS, alarms, CloudFormation stack
