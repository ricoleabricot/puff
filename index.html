📜 Preamble: why this course exists

Almost every piece of software we use runs in the cloud, yet you can finish an engineering degree without ever deploying something past your laptop, isolating a network or looking at a cloud bill, and this course closes that gap.

AWS Academy comes with its own “Cloud Architecting course”.
It's solid, but it runs about 60 hours, stays generic and talks more about AWS services than about architecture.

So we will use this one instead: shorter, opinionated, built around one small cloud you grow step by step, and full of real-life stories from running things at Alan, my current company.
AWS's labs and sandbox stay, they're the best playground we could ask for.

🧭 How the course works

Each course mixes two things: concepts in plain words, and hands-on where you build them right away.
You don't need any work experience: if you know a bit of Linux, networking and Docker, you're ready.

The hands-on happens in AWS, never on a personal account, and nobody pays anything.

📖

Think

The thread running through it all is Puff ☁️, your own small cloud.

Course after course you give it what every cloud needs: storage, servers, a database, a private network, badges and keys, alarms, the ability to grow on its own, and finally a recipe to rebuild it all in one command.

Puff needs a customer from day one, so it gets a first tenant: the board app, a tiny message board where people post short messages and everyone sees them.
It's boring on purpose, we care about the cloud around it.
